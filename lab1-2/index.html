street patterns

This map focuses on Capitol Hill’s streets and blocks. Road names stay visible. Use the checkboxes to compare the view with and without place and transit labels.

Dusk lighting and hidden points of interest are the lab’s starting settings. Both additional label settings start off; their effects depend on the location and zoom.

Compare labels

1. Hide place labels

showPlaceLabels: false hides place-name labels, including neighborhood and city names. Fewer labels can make block shapes easier to compare, but they also remove geographic context. Restore place labels when locating an area within Seattle or exploring an unfamiliar neighborhood.

2. Hide transit labels

showTransitLabels: false hides transit icons and text, keeping the initial view focused on street geometry. Restore these labels when looking for transit. Assessing service or accessibility also requires appropriate transit data; street patterns alone cannot answer those questions.

Pan and zoom to explore. “Return to Capitol Hill” restores the starting view and keeps your label choices. Use the compass to reset north and the fullscreen button to expand the map. Basemap: Mapbox and its data providers. This map does not calculate routes or measure accessibility.
