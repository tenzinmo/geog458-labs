Capitol Hill
street patterns

This map emphasizes Capitol Hill’s street pattern. It retains road labels for orientation while reducing competing place and transit labels.

The starting view uses dusk lighting and hides points of interest. Two additional changes shape what the map emphasizes.

1. Hide place labels

showPlaceLabels: false hides place names, including neighborhood and city labels. This clears space to compare the arrangement of streets and blocks within the area identified by the page title. It is useful for a neighborhood-scale overview, but removes regional context when zooming out.

2. Hide transit labels

showTransitLabels: false hides transit icons and text. This helps isolate the street pattern for an initial overview of the built environment. The tradeoff is missing information about stops and stations, so these labels should be restored for transit planning or an accessibility study.

Pan and zoom to explore. The compass resets north; the fullscreen control expands the map. Basemap features come from Mapbox and its data providers. This page does not calculate routes or measure accessibility.
