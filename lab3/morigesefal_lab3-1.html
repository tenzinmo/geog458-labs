Sources, method & limits

Source: U.S. EPA AirData / AQS, PM2.5 parameter 88101. All observations use July 5, 2025, “24-HR BLK AVG,” and the same units. One eligible instrument is retained per site; station IDs remain text.

QGIS IDW uses inverse-distance weights with power 2, projected station coordinates in UTM zone 10N, and a requested 250 m grid. Cell size controls the raster, not the accuracy of the monitoring network. See the processing record for the actual resulting grid spacing.

The thematic tiles are a historical snapshot. Local PNG requests are limited to the exported extent and zooms 8–12. The page requests no live Mapbox basemap tiles; MapLibre JS/CSS still load from a CDN.

A classroom interpolation is not an official air-quality forecast, AQI category, health assessment, or measurement at every pixel. Empty areas outside the interpolation remain transparent.
