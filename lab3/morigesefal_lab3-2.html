Sources & interpretation

Canopy: University of Vermont Spatial Analysis Laboratory / City of Seattle, 2021 assessment. Parks: Seattle Parks and Recreation inventory, retrieved October 23, 2026.

This is a historical, area-level comparison for residents exploring Seattle’s urban environment. It does not measure present shade, public access, or canopy within a park. Park points are centroids, not entrances.

The canopy classification was manually reviewed; source metadata reports no formal accuracy assessment. A 20 m raster cell is a display choice, not a claim of measurement accuracy.
